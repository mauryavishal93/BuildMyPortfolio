SERVICE INFORMATION

Terms of Service

These terms describe the current free tools and recurring Pro subscriptions. Review the terms and refund information before purchasing.

Current service

BuildMyPortfolio provides browser-based resume editing, templates, basic ATS readiness checks, cover-letter drafting, standard PDF export, and JSON backup tools. You can use core editor features without payment or signing in.

Drafts and cloud storage

Browser drafts are stored locally. Optional Google sign-in and Firebase cloud resume storage are provided separately as described in the Privacy Policy. Keep your own backups of important work.

ATS and generated drafts

ATS results are deterministic estimates, not a prediction or guarantee of how an employer's system will evaluate a resume. Cover-letter drafts should be reviewed and corrected before use.

Pro subscriptions

The proposed Pro plans are ₹99 per month and ₹399 per year, billed on a recurring basis until canceled. Cancellation stops future renewals; paid access continues until the end of the active billing period. Purchases require a signed-in Google account and are processed by Razorpay. The site stores the subscription status against that account; resume contents are not sent to Razorpay.

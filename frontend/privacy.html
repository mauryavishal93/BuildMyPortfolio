Privacy Policy

This policy explains how BuildMyPortfolio handles information when you use this website and its resume tools.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

1. Who we are and what this covers

BuildMyPortfolio is a browser-based resume editor operated by its developer, Vishal Maurya. This policy applies to the BuildMyPortfolio website, editor, and related features. Contact mauryavishal93@gmail.com with privacy questions.

2. Resume information stays in your browser

The information you enter into your resume, including contact details, work history, education, skills, and any job description you provide, is processed in your browser. The editor automatically saves your project and interface preferences in your browser's local storage so they are available when you return using that browser.

BuildMyPortfolio does not require an account and does not send your resume, job description, or generated cover letter to an app server or AI service. Analysis, cover-letter drafting, PDF previews, PDF exports, and JSON imports and exports are performed in your browser. The app does not sync this information between devices.

3. Information handled when you visit

Your browser must request website files from the hosting provider for the site to load. The hosting infrastructure may process technical request information, such as your IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, and request timestamps, in connection with delivering, securing, and maintaining the website. The operator does not receive your resume contents through the editor's normal operation. The hosting provider's processing is subject to its own terms and privacy practices.

The app does not intentionally use advertising trackers, analytics services, or its own tracking cookies. Your browser may store the editor's data in local storage; this is browser storage, not a cookie.

4. Feedback and communications

If you choose the feedback form on the About page, it opens a new message in your device's email application. The form does not submit your message to this website. Your email application or provider processes the message only if you review and send it. If you contact the developer directly, the information in your message and email address will be used to respond and handle your request.

5. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell or rent personal information. Resume information is not shared by the app with third parties. Website hosting providers may process technical request information as described above. If you follow a link to an external website, that service's privacy policy and practices apply. Your email provider will also handle any message you choose to send.

6. Retention, security, and your choices

Resume data and preferences remain in your browser's local storage until you remove them or your browser clears them. You can delete the saved project by clearing this website's local storage or site data in your browser settings. Export a JSON backup first if you want to keep a copy. Deleting browser data does not delete a JSON file you previously downloaded or information in messages you sent.

Protect access to your device and browser profile, especially on shared devices. Local storage is subject to the security of your device and browser. No method of storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children and applicable rights

BuildMyPortfolio is a general-purpose resume tool and is not directed to children. The app does not ask users to provide personal information to an account or server. Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights regarding personal information processed by website hosting or in communications you send. Contact the developer to ask about a request; information stored only in your browser can be managed directly through your browser settings.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the website or its data practices change. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page. Please review it periodically.

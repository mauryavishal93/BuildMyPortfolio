YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how BuildMyPortfolio handles information when you use this website and its resume tools.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Who we are and what this covers

BuildMyPortfolio is a browser-based resume editor operated by its developer, Vishal Maurya. This policy applies to the BuildMyPortfolio website, editor, and related features. Contact mauryavishal93@gmail.com with privacy questions.

2. Resume information and optional cloud saving

You can use the resume editor without signing in. Resume information, job descriptions, cover-letter drafts, analysis, previews, and exports are processed in your browser. The editor saves the current project and interface preferences in your browser's local storage.

If you choose to sign in with Google and save a resume to the cloud, the app uses Firebase Authentication to process your Google account identifier and available profile information, such as your email address and display name. The resume projects you choose to save—including contact details, work history, education, skills, and any profile photo—are stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore under your account so you can access them on other devices. Editing a cloud resume syncs later changes to that saved project. Job descriptions and generated cover-letter drafts are not included in cloud resume saves.

Cloud saving is optional. Your browser copy remains separate from cloud copies. Signing out does not delete cloud resumes; use the cloud-resume controls to delete individual saved resumes. The hosting and Firebase services process information as described by their own terms and privacy practices.

3. Information handled when you visit

Your browser must request website files from the hosting provider for the site to load. The hosting infrastructure may process technical request information, such as your IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, and request timestamps, in connection with delivering, securing, and maintaining the website. The operator does not receive resume contents through the editor's normal operation unless you choose to save a project to Firebase. The hosting provider's processing is subject to its own terms and privacy practices.

The app does not intentionally use advertising trackers, analytics services, or its own tracking cookies. Your browser may store the editor's data in local storage; this is browser storage, not a cookie.

4. Feedback and communications

If you choose the feedback form on the About page, it opens a new message in your device's email application. The form does not submit your message to this website. Your email application or provider processes the message only if you review and send it. If you contact the developer directly, the information in your message and email address will be used to respond and handle your request.

5. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell or rent personal information. If you use Google sign-in and cloud saving, Google Firebase processes sign-in information and the resume data you choose to store there to provide those features. Website hosting providers may process technical request information as described above. If you follow a link to an external website, that service's privacy policy and practices apply. Your email provider will also handle any message you choose to send.

6. Retention, security, and your choices

Browser-stored resume data and preferences remain in local storage until you remove them or your browser clears them. You can remove them by clearing this website's local storage or site data in your browser settings. Cloud resumes remain stored in Firebase until you delete them using the cloud-resume controls or contact the developer to request deletion. Signing out or clearing browser data does not delete cloud resumes. Export a JSON backup first if you want to keep a copy. Deleting browser data or a cloud resume does not delete a JSON file you previously downloaded or information in messages you sent.

Protect access to your device and browser profile, especially on shared devices. Local storage is subject to the security of your device and browser. No method of storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children and applicable rights

BuildMyPortfolio is a general-purpose resume tool and is not directed to children. Signing in is optional. Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights regarding personal information processed by website hosting, Firebase, or in communications you send. Contact the developer to ask about a request; information stored only in your browser can be managed through your browser settings, and cloud resumes can be managed in the app.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the website or its data practices change. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page. Please review it periodically.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how PersonaCraftPro handles information when you use this website and its resume tools.

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Who we are and what this covers

PersonaCraftPro is a browser-based resume editor operated by MVTechnology-labs. This policy applies to the PersonaCraftPro website, editor, and related features. Contact buildymyportfolio@gmail.com with privacy questions.

2. Resume information and optional cloud saving

You can use the resume editor without signing in. Resume information, job descriptions, cover-letter drafts, analysis, previews, and exports are processed in your browser. For guest users, the editor's Save in this browser preference is on by default and controls whether the current resume is autosaved in local storage. When it is off, resume edits remain in memory and are discarded when the page is refreshed or closed. The preference and interface settings may still be stored locally.

When you sign in with Google, the app uses Firebase Authentication to process your Google account identifier and available profile information, such as your email address and display name. Resume projects—including contact details, work history, education, skills, and any profile photo—are stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore under your account, and edits sync to the cloud. The editor also stores the ID of the cloud resume you last opened in a private account preference so it can reopen that resume on your next visit. If that resume no longer exists, it opens the most recently updated resume instead. If the account has no saved resumes, the current guest draft may be added to the account. Authenticated resume data is not saved to guest resume storage. Job descriptions and generated cover-letter drafts are not included in cloud resume saves.

All templates, PDF exports, and ATS checks are available without a subscription or usage counter. Resume contents and job descriptions remain in your browser unless you choose to save a resume to Firebase.

Google sign-in and cloud storage are optional. Guest browser saving is separate from cloud storage; changing the browser-saving preference does not delete cloud resumes. Signing out does not delete cloud resumes; use the cloud-resume controls to delete individual saved resumes. The hosting and Firebase services process information as described by their own terms and privacy practices.

3. Existing billing records

New subscriptions and voucher redemptions are unavailable. For existing subscriptions, the server may retain your account identifier, subscription identifier, billing status, and paid-through date in Firebase to display billing history and process cancellation. Payment details are handled by the payment provider under its own terms and privacy practices. Resume contents and job descriptions are not sent to payment providers. Existing billing records are retained as needed to support account history and cancellation.

4. Information handled when you visit

Your browser must request website files from the hosting provider for the site to load. The hosting infrastructure may process technical request information, such as your IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, and request timestamps, in connection with delivering, securing, and maintaining the website. The operator does not receive resume contents through the editor's normal operation unless you choose to save a project to Firebase. The hosting provider's processing is subject to its own terms and privacy practices.

The app does not intentionally use advertising trackers, analytics services, or its own tracking cookies. Your browser may store the editor's data in local storage; this is browser storage, not a cookie.

5. Feedback and communications

If you choose the feedback form on the About page, it opens a new message in your device's email application. The form does not submit your message to this website. Your email application or provider processes the message only if you review and send it. If you contact the developer directly, the information in your message and email address will be used to respond and handle your request.

6. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell or rent personal information. If you use Google sign-in, Google Firebase processes sign-in information and the resume data you choose to store. Website hosting providers may process technical request information as described above. If you follow a link to an external website, that service's privacy policy and practices apply. Your email provider will also handle any message you choose to send.

7. Retention, security, and your choices

When guest browser saving is on, the resume and preferences remain in local storage until you turn saving off, remove them, or your browser clears them. Turning browser saving off removes the stored guest resume; clearing this website's local storage or site data also removes browser-stored data. Cloud resumes remain stored in Firebase until you delete them using the cloud-resume controls or contact the developer to request deletion. Signing out or clearing browser data does not delete cloud resumes. Export a JSON backup first if you want to keep a copy. Deleting browser data or a cloud resume does not delete a JSON file you previously downloaded or information in messages you sent.

Protect access to your device and browser profile, especially on shared devices. Local storage is subject to the security of your device and browser. No method of storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children and applicable rights

PersonaCraftPro is a general-purpose resume tool and is not directed to children. Signing in is optional. Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights regarding personal information processed by website hosting, Firebase, or in communications you send. Contact the developer to ask about a request; information stored only in your browser can be managed through your browser settings, and cloud resumes can be managed in the app.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the website or its data practices change. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page. Please review it periodically.

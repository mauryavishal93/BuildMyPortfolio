Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to help you get started with confidence.

Is PersonaCraftPro free to use?

The core editor is free to use. Visit the Plans page for current feature details and paid options.

Can I download my resume as a PDF?

Standard PDF export is included. Some Pro-template downloads have plan limits; check the Plans page for current details.

Are the templates ATS-friendly?

Templates are designed with readable resume structure in mind. ATS systems vary, so no template can guarantee a particular screening outcome.

Can I import my existing resume?

Yes. Choose Import resume to bring a supported file into the editor and review the imported content before applying it.

Can I save and edit my resume later?

Use browser saving or sign in with Google for account-based cloud resume sync.

How is my resume information handled?

Review the Privacy Policy for information about data, storage, and your choices.

FREE RESUME BUILDER

BuildMyPortfolio

A free workspace for building, arranging, and exporting a polished resume.

Tools for your next resume

Start with one of 17 layouts, from ATS-friendly and compact formats to two-column and photo templates. Then shape the content to fit your experience.

  • Edit your profile, experience, education, skills, projects, and custom sections.
  • Reorder sections, add entries, and choose structured flow or free positioning.
  • Adjust fonts, colors, spacing, alignment, margins, photos, and page numbers.
  • Preview A4 pages and export a PDF with selectable text and clickable contact links.

Save a portable JSON project to back up or move your resume to another browser.

Private by default

Your draft is autosaved in this browser's local storage. Resume data is not uploaded to an account or synchronized between devices. Use JSON export for a portable backup.

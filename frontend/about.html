ABOUT THE PRODUCT

BuildMyPortfolio

A focused workspace for building, arranging, and exporting a polished resume.

What you can do

Choose from 17 resume templates, edit profile details and sections, adjust typography and spacing, and arrange content in structured or free-position mode.

Export a searchable, selectable-text PDF, or save and load a portable JSON project. Email and website links remain clickable in the resume and PDF.

Your resume data

Your draft is autosaved in this browser. It is not uploaded to a user account or synchronized between devices. Use JSON export when you need a portable backup.

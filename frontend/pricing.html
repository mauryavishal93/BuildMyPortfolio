FeatureFreePro

Pricing, purchase, and access terms

Pro is a recurring subscription: ₹99 per month or ₹399 per year. The subscription renews at the selected interval until canceled. Canceling stops future renewals; access remains through the current paid period. Payments are handled by Razorpay. Resume content is not sent to Razorpay.

Purchases require signing in with Google. Core editing, local saving, JSON backup, resume-only ATS checks, cover letters, and standard PDF export remain free. A Google account can export two resumes using Pro templates at no cost; each Pro-template PDF download counts, including repeat downloads.

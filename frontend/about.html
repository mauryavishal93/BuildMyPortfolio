Resume tools

  • Choose from 25 templates and customize sections, layout, and styling.
  • Check general ATS readiness or compare skills and experience with a job description.
  • Draft an editable cover letter using your resume and a target role.
  • Preview A4 pages and export a text-selectable PDF.
  • Sign in with Google to sync resumes to your private cloud library and continue editing them across devices.

You can also download or load a JSON backup whenever you need to move a project.

Choose how guest drafts are saved

When you are not signed in, the editor's Save in this browser toggle is on by default and controls whether your resume is autosaved in this browser. Turn it off to keep edits only in memory; refreshing or closing the page then discards them. Your preference is remembered. When you sign in, resume edits save to your private Google account cloud library regardless of that toggle. The editor reopens the cloud resume you last worked on; if it no longer exists, the most recently updated resume opens instead. If your account has no saved resumes, your guest draft can be added to the account. You can manage cloud resumes in the editor.
